What the common fresher tests look like

TCS NQTVerbal, reasoning and numerical (Foundation), then advanced quant and coding for Digital and Prime. Followed by a combined technical, managerial and HR interview.
Wipro Elite NTHAptitude, a written essay and coding in one online test, then a technical interview and an HR interview.
Tech MahindraFive stages: aptitude, a technical or psychometric round, an AI communication assessment, a technical interview, then HR.
Cognizant GenCAptitude for GenC. GenC Next and Elevate add a coding round. Then technical and HR interviews.
Product companiesAn online test with 2–3 DSA problems, then 2–4 interviews on DSA, CS fundamentals (OS, DBMS, networks) and projects.
Core GETBranch-subject test (thermo, machines, circuits, process), often GATE-style, then technical and HR interviews. Some PSUs use the GATE score directly.
